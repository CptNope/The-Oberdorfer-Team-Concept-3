# WordPress build v0.10.1

- **Follow-up emails (formerly "Sequences") are easier to edit, with no technical knowledge needed.**
  - **Per series:** an On/Off switch and one plain choice, "Wait for my OK" or "Send it on its own".
  - **Timeline:** the emails read as a timeline: "Right away", "2 days later", "7 days later". Click one to edit it.
  - **When it goes out:** "Send it [2] days after they start", with an optional "Then keep sending it every [30] days".
  - **What kind of email:** three choices, each explained. "I write it". "Monthly town update", built from the town guides. "AI drafts it", from your instructions, and the draft waits for your OK.
  - **The message:** plain text, with a blank line between paragraphs. Personal details go in with buttons ("First name", "Agent phone", "Buying guide link") and show as [First name] placeholders, never as codes.
  - **Live preview:** each message shows as a sample person, Dana, would receive it.
  - **Adding and removing:** "Add an email" and "Remove this email"; nothing changes until you save.
  - **Emails with custom formatting** keep an HTML box, so nothing is lost.
- The saved emails, their timing and their sending rules are unchanged.
