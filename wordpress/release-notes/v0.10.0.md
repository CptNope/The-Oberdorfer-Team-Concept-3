# WordPress build v0.10.0

- **Leads, redesigned:** every Leads screen now opens in one branded workspace inside wp-admin, with a navy menu, an ivory page and the team's typefaces. It works on phones as well as desktops.
  - **Today is home.** The Leads menu opens on it. One sentence says who to answer first ("Three people are waiting. Start with Dana."). Below it are three lists: waiting for a first reply, follow-ups due, and back on the site.
  - **Every row can be answered from Today.** The person's preferred way to reach them comes first (Call, Text or Email). **Log** opens the one-tap outcomes and books the next follow-up.
  - **Side column:** this week's follow-ups, reply speed over the last 30 days, and emails waiting for approval.
  - **Same look everywhere:** All leads, the lead screen, Pipeline, Approvals, Reports, Sequences, Newsletter and Settings share it.
  - **Lead screen:** Call, Text and Email sit beside the name, with stage, intent, type, agent and waiting time as tags. Log a touch comes first.
  - **Filters:** Today and Pipeline use Everyone / Team / Brandon / Kaitlyn buttons.
  - **Town names:** they read as names ("Worcester, Holden") wherever a lead's towns are listed.
  - **Phones:** the menu becomes a strip along the top, rows stack, buttons grow to finger size, and the pipeline scrolls one column at a time.
- No data, routing, email or permission changes.
