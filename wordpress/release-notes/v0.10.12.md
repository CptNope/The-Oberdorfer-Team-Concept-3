**Agents now lists only Brandon and Kaitlyn.**

The joint "Brandon & Kaitlyn Oberdorfer" page was an entry in Agents, so WordPress showed three agents, and the agent pickers on listings and testimonials offered it as a third person. It is now an ordinary page under Our Team, at **/team/brandon-kaitlyn-oberdorfer/**, and looks exactly the same.

- **Moved automatically, once.** On the first visit to wp-admin after updating, the joint profile becomes the page: its introduction, its *Wakefield at Home* paragraphs and its search title come along, and the old entry goes to Trash (it can be restored). The old address, /agents/brandon-kaitlyn-oberdorfer/, redirects to the new one.
- **Editing it:** Pages › Our Team › Brandon & Kaitlyn Oberdorfer, or the link in the dashboard's "Start here" box. A note above the editor says what the page is.
- **"The whole team" is still an option**, without a person behind it: listings keep "Brandon & Kaitlyn (the team)", testimonials keep "Both (shown on each profile)", and team leads still go to both of them.
- On the joint page, the current-listings strip's "all listings" link now goes to the team listings on the Buy page.
