# WordPress build v0.10.26

**Self-hosted fonts and an empty Journal kept out of search.**

- **Fonts from the site itself:** Cormorant Garamond (300 to 600, italics 300 and 400) and Manrope (variable) are WOFF2 files in the theme, Latin subset, under the SIL Open Font License. The two most used are preloaded. This removes the remaining render-blocking request to Google Fonts and the connection to Google on every page; the privacy policy is updated to match.
- **Journal:** while it has no published article it is marked noindex and left out of the sitemap (an empty list reads to Google as a thin page). The first published article brings it back automatically.
- Also done on the live site the same day: Breeze's "Preload Links" turned off, and Cloudflare's Email Address Obfuscation turned off.
