# WordPress build v0.10.25

**Closer photo sizes for phones.**

- Theme photos now come in 640, 800 and 960 pixel copies (the home page's main photo had no 960 copy), so a phone downloads a size close to what it shows. After 0.10.24, mobile PageSpeed rose from 86 to 89 and the main photo appeared in 3.6 s instead of 4.1 s; it still found the photos about 2.5 times larger than needed.
