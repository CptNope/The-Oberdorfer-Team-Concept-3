Lead capture and nurture: the new **Oberdorfer Leads** plugin (`ot-leads.zip`). Pre-release for staging. Setup: `docs/leads-setup.md`.

## What it does
- **Every form saves.** Showing requests, agent and town-guide questions, buyer, seller, office and recruiting forms, the newsletter, and two new sign-ups: "Email me when [agent] lists a new home" on each agent's listings page, and a monthly update on each town guide. Also an open-house sign-in page for a tablet at the door: `/?ot_signin=<listing id>`.
- **Spam protection:** Cloudflare Turnstile (once keys are set), a hidden trap field and a rate limit. No puzzles for real people.
- **Consent:** an unticked "email me updates" box; the exact wording, page and time are stored. Sign-up forms show their wording above the field.
- **Routing:** the listing agent for showings and open houses, the named agent on their own pages, the town's owner for town guides, then Brandon and Kaitlyn. A second message from the same email joins the existing lead.
- **Alerts:** an instant email to the agent with the lead and an **I'm on it** button. If nobody marks the lead contacted within 15 minutes in business hours, Brandon and Kaitlyn are alerted. The person gets a short "we have your message" email from their agent.
- **Leads menu:** Inbox (views: New today, Waiting for a reply, Warm, Team inbox; filters, search, bulk assign, CSV export), lead screen (contact, consent, where they came from, timeline of forms, visits and emails, notes, calls, tasks, nurture), pipeline Board (drag between stages), Approval queue, Sequences, Newsletter, Settings. A Leads panel on the dashboard.
- **Nurture:** New buyer, New seller, Relocation, Long-term and Past-client sequences (editable). Emails wait for approval unless a team leader switches a sequence to automatic, send 8 a.m. to 8 p.m. Monday to Saturday, at most one every 3 days, from the agent's name with replies to the agent, with the office address and one-click unsubscribe.
- **Monthly town updates** from the guides' sourced market figures and the team's listings there; **new-listing alerts** drafted when a team listing is published; the **At Home newsletter** composer.
- **Email through Brevo** (API key in wp-config.php or Settings); bounces, complaints and unsubscribes come back by webhook and are never emailed again. Mailboxes stay on Rackspace.
- **Outside CRM (optional):** a signed webhook (Zapier, Make, most CRMs) or Follow Up Boss, with retries. Built-in only by default.
- **AI (off until a key is added):** Claude drafts "AI draft" steps and lead summaries from the lead's record and the guides only; phone numbers and emails never reach it; every draft is checked for unsourced numbers and fair-housing terms before it can be approved.
- **Privacy:** agents see only their own leads (role "Team agent", linked under Users); every view and export is logged; Tools > Export and Erase Personal Data include leads; unconverted leads with no activity are deleted after 24 months (setting).

## Install or upgrade
1. Upload `oberdorfer.zip` and `ot-core.zip` ("Replace current with uploaded").
2. Upload and activate `ot-leads.zip`. **Once active, the forms are live:** alerts go to the team leaders in Leads > Settings (Brandon's and Kaitlyn's Gmail by default). Change them to your own address while testing on staging.
3. Follow `docs/leads-setup.md`: Rackspace mailboxes, Brevo and its DNS records, Turnstile, the server cron.
