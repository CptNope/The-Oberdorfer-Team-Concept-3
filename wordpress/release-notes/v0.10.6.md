# WordPress build v0.10.6

**WCAG 2.2 AA audit and an Accessibility statement.**

The live site was audited on November 15, 2026: axe-core on every page type (home, buy, sell, market, team, journal, join, contact, privacy, communities, town and neighborhood guides, agent profiles, office, 404), then keyboard, focus visibility, 320px reflow and form-error checks by hand.

Fixed:
- **Link in the footer told apart by color only** (WCAG 1.4.1, serious, every page). Footer legal links are now underlined.
- **Town price tables overflowed at 320px** (1.4.10). The two-year price table now scrolls inside a labeled region, like the market table.
- **Footer newsletter field had a faint focus indicator** (2.4.7). It now shows the standard focus outline.
- **Sidebars nested as complementary landmarks** (best practice). Town guide and listing sidebars are labeled sections; agent sidebars are plain groups.
- **Community card overlay** (preventive, 1.4.3). The gradient is darker where the text sits, so it keeps 4.5:1 when photos replace the illustrations.

Passed: images and alternatives, headings and landmarks, form labels, required fields, autocomplete, grouped radio buttons, error messages tied to their fields and announced, focus visible on every control, focus not hidden by the sticky header, skip link, reduced motion, target size, language.

Known limitation: the Wakefield at Home PDF is untagged. The statement says so and offers the contents in another format.

New page: **Accessibility statement** at /accessibility/, written from `tools/data/accessibility-statement.json`. The footer link and the contact page note point to it.
