Makes the site edit like a page, for a team new to WordPress. Pre-release for staging.

## Edit on the page
Open any guide, profile or listing: the editor shows the whole page as visitors see it, with a short note at the top on how it works.
- **Agent profiles:** click the role line, the summary or the biography and type. Phone, title, specialties, recognition and the headshot are in **Agent details** (open "Meta Boxes" at the bottom of the screen).
- **Town and neighborhood guides:** click the headline or the summary and type. The research sections (facts, prices, commute, sources) are kept separate so they stay accurate; click one to see where it comes from.
- **Listings:** type the address as the title and write the description on the page. Price, status, rooms and extra photos are in **Listing details**; the main photo is the featured image in the sidebar.

Under the hood these are core blocks bound to each post's own fields with the **Block Bindings API** (`core/post-meta`). The templates hold the layout; the posts hold only their own words and data.

## Also
- A **Start here** panel on the dashboard links the common tasks: edit a profile, add a listing, show or hide guides, the IDX switch.
- Comments are hidden (the site has none) and Posts are labelled **Journal**.
- Section settings are hidden on guides, profiles and listings, so a click can't change a page's layout by accident. In the Site Editor each section is labelled.
- **Re-running the importer keeps edits.** It remembers what it last wrote to each field and only updates fields nobody has changed since. The research data always refreshes.

## Upgrade from 0.2.x
Upload both zips again ("Replace current with uploaded"), then purge Breeze and Cloudflare. No re-import is needed.

If anyone has edited the guide, agent or listing templates in **Appearance > Editor**, those saved copies override the new ones: reset them there (Templates > the template > "Reset").
