# WordPress build v0.10.8

**Online bookings become leads.**

When someone books a call with Brandon or Kaitlyn (JAT Calendar Sync), the CRM records it:

- **New person:** a new "Booked call" lead, assigned to the agent they booked, at "Appointment set".
- **Someone already in the CRM** (matched by email): the same lead. A team lead is assigned to the booked agent; an agent's lead keeps its owner. Missing name and phone are filled in, and an early-stage lead moves to "Appointment set".
- **Timeline and Today:** the booking (time, type, video link, answers, notes) is on the timeline, and the call is a task on the booked agent's Today screen at the booked time. Moving or cancelling the booking, by the visitor or in Google Calendar, moves or removes the task and is logged.
- **No extra email:** the booking plugin already confirms with the visitor and tells the agent. A booking is not marketing consent, so no follow-up sequence starts. An agent's own CRM receives it like any inquiry.

Also: the "Waiting for a first reply" badge shows for New leads only (a booked call isn't waiting).

Needs JAT Calendar Sync 2.1.0 (People); the build in `wordpress/dist/` also fires the move action for changes made in Google Calendar.
