# WordPress build v0.7.0

Testimonials and reviews, done within the law and the review sites' terms.

## ot-core 0.7.0
- Testimonials (new menu): a client's words show on their agent's profile only once the client agreed to be quoted on this website and REWAP approved it, word for word, with a link to the original review. Each screen says what is still needed.
- "Reviews on other sites" (Agent details): links to an agent's reviews on RateMyAgent, Zillow or Google under "Client stories", without copying them. Brandon's 13 RateMyAgent reviews are linked.
- Brandon's 13 RateMyAgent reviews are added as hidden draft testimonials (title, site, link; no text) for the team to ask each client.
- The Client stories section is left out of a profile with neither, instead of showing an empty state. No review or rating markup is published.

## Theme 0.7.0
- The agent profile's Client stories pattern now holds the ot-core section block.

## ot-leads 0.7.0
- Version number only.

## Why it works this way
- Reviews on RateMyAgent, Zillow and Google belong to those sites, so they are linked, not copied.
- Quoting a client needs their permission to be quoted here; the brokerage approves testimonials in advertising.
- The FTC rule on consumer reviews and testimonials (16 CFR 465) bars fake or misleading reviews; quotes are exact and dated.
- Google shows no review stars for a business's reviews of itself, so no review markup is added.

## Install
Upload `oberdorfer.zip`, `ot-core.zip` and `ot-leads.zip` and choose "Replace current with uploaded". Open the dashboard once: that adds Brandon's review link and the draft testimonials.
