# WordPress build v0.9.0: a stronger built-in CRM

Oberdorfer Leads gets a daily workflow, return-visit signals, reports, safeguards and per-agent CRM support. Neither Brandon nor Kaitlyn uses an outside CRM, so the built-in one stays the system; the per-agent part waits until one does.

## Agent workflow
- **Leads > Today:** new leads waiting for a first reply, follow-ups due today and overdue, people back on the site (last 3 days), and the week ahead. Team leaders can view any agent.
- **One-tap logging** on every row and on the lead screen: Called, Voicemail, No answer, Texted, Emailed, Met, They replied. It counts as the first reply, closes the task and books the next follow-up automatically (tomorrow after a voicemail or no answer, 3 days after a text or email), or when chosen.
- **Morning email** per person, with their own and the team's leads together.

## Smarter follow-up
- **Signals:** someone who sent a form comes back after a week or more, looks at a team listing, opens a new town guide (added to their towns, so their monthly update covers it), or views three pages in a day. The agent gets a call task and an email, at most every 3 days per lead.
- **Quiet leads:** a contacted, appointment or active lead with no activity for 14 days gets a check-in task.
- **Home anniversary:** a yearly check-in from the closing day, with an offer of a recent-sales review (approval queue like every sequence).

## Each agent's CRM (optional)
- Settings > Each agent's CRM: a webhook (Zapier, Make, any CRM) or Follow Up Boss key per agent. Their leads are forwarded on arrival and on reassignment, with retries.
- "Follow-up emails: sent by their own CRM" stops the site's sequences for their leads, so nobody gets two sets.
- Unsubscribes on the site are sent to their webhook; a CRM can send unsubscribes and stage changes back to `/wp-json/ot-leads/v1/crm-update` (bearer token on the card).

## Reports and safeguards
- **Leads > Reports:** inquiries and sign-ups, median time to first reply and % within the response window, by agent, form, referrer, campaign, page and town, current stages, by month.
- **Duplicates:** same phone or same name under two emails; merge moves the whole history onto one lead.
- **Health check:** the scheduler, email (Brevo, failures), CRM deliveries, escalated leads, drafts waiting, duplicates; on Today, the dashboard, Settings and Tools > Site Health.

## Privacy policy
- Two sentences updated (town interest from guide visits; a CRM is told about unsubscribes). Staging's page is updated to match.
