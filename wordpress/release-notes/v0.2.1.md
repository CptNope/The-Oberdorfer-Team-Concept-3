Makes every part of an agent profile editable in WordPress. Pre-release for staging.

## Editing a profile
Go to **Agents**, open Brandon or Kaitlyn, and edit:
- **The biography:** the main editor at the top (120 to 250 words, first person). While it is empty, the page shows the "biography to be supplied" placeholder.
- **Agent details** (the box below the editor):
  - role, professional title, one-sentence summary
  - direct phone (the Call button dials it) and email
  - specialties, languages, service area
  - MA license number
  - recognition, one per line, written as `Award | Source`
  - digital card link
  - headshot (replaces the interim crop of the team portrait) and photo caption

The box starts filled with what the page shows today. An empty field shows the [bracketed placeholder] with its "To confirm" badge, so nothing unconfirmed appears as fact. An emptied field stays empty.

The joint "Brandon & Kaitlyn" profile's introduction is its main editor. While empty, it shows the three paragraphs from *Wakefield at Home*.

**Re-running the importer never overwrites these edits.** It only refreshes the underlying research data.
