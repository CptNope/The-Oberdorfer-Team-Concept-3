Agent listing pages, and listing pages built to turn visits into showings. Pre-release for staging.

## Each agent's listings page
`/agents/kaitlyn-oberdorfer/listings/` (and the same for every agent):
- Current listings (for sale, coming soon, under agreement), then recently sold.
- Towns served: the town guides ticked in **Agent details > Towns served**.
- Message and Call buttons for that agent.
- It fills itself: a listing appears on the page of its **Listing agent** and its **Co-listing agent** (new field). The joint Brandon & Kaitlyn page lists every team listing.
- Profiles show a "Current listings" strip linking to it.

It lives inside oberdorferteam.com, not on a separate domain or subdomain, so every agent page builds the one site's search standing, and each listing keeps a single address that agent pages link to.

**Search:** its own title ("Kaitlyn Oberdorfer's Listings | The Oberdorfer Team"), description and canonical; breadcrumbs (Home › Our Team › Kaitlyn Oberdorfer › Listings); CollectionPage and ItemList structured data; added to the sitemap and `/llms.txt` once it has listings, and marked noindex while it has none.

## Listing pages
New in **Listing details** (grouped into The home, Costs, Open house, Video/3D/social, Floor plan and brochure, Agents, On the site):
- **Video tour** (YouTube or Vimeo) and **3D tour** (Matterport, iGuide, Kuula or CloudPano): shown in a "Tour the home" section. Nothing loads from YouTube or Matterport until the visitor presses play, so pages stay fast and private. Links from other sites are refused, with a note in the box.
- **Facebook, Instagram and TikTok posts:** "See it on ..." buttons. Embedding them would require Meta's app token and load their tracking scripts, so they link instead.
- **Floor plan** (image shown on the page, or a PDF download) and **property brochure** (PDF).
- **Open house start and end:** the page writes "Sunday, December 6, 1 to 3 pm", offers **Add to my calendar** (.ics for iPhone and Outlook, plus Google Calendar) and hides it once it is over.
- **Property tax and condo fee:** prefill a **payment estimate** (price, down payment, the visitor's own interest rate, term, taxes, fee, insurance). The site never states an interest rate.
- Under the gallery: **Request a showing**, **Watch the tour**, and **Share** (the phone's share sheet, copy link, Facebook, email, text). No share scripts or trackers.
- On phones, a bar at the foot of the screen keeps **Call** and **Request a showing** in reach.
- The agent card beside the showing form shows the listing agent's own photo and links to their profile.
