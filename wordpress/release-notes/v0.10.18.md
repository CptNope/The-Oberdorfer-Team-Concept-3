# WordPress build v0.10.18

**A place for the team's mortgage lender, built to the advertising rules.**

- **Settings > Lender** (ot-core) holds the lender the team introduces buyers to: Brian Tankersley, VP & Sales Manager, Security First Mortgage Funding, LLC. The details come from his email to the team and his page at securityfirstmf.com, and can be edited there.
- **Buy page, Financing section** (block `ot/lender`, pattern `preferred-lender`): his name and title, his NMLS ID (32634), the company's NMLS ID (252771) and Massachusetts Mortgage Broker and Lender license (MC252771), each linked to NMLS Consumer Access, his office, mobile, email and address, and a link to his page.
- **The disclosure under it, always printed:** you are free to choose any lender; using him is not a condition of working with the team; the team and REWAP receive nothing of value for the introduction (or, if chosen, that the lender pays its proportional share of shared advertising); the lender is a separate company; approval, rates and terms come from the lender; Equal Housing Opportunity.
- **No rates, APRs, payments or loan terms** are shown anywhere (Regulation Z).
- **Hidden until approved.** The section renders nothing until the team records the business arrangement and ticks that REWAP and the lender approved it. In the editor it shows what is still missing.

Rules behind it: RESPA section 8 (12 CFR 1024.14), the SAFE Act and NMLS ID rules, Massachusetts 209 CMR 42.00 (license number in advertising), Regulation Z (12 CFR 1026.24) and fair lending.
