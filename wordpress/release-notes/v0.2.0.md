Two new controls for the team: a show/hide switch on every community guide, and the team's own listings. Pre-release for staging.

## Show or hide guides
**Communities > Show or hide guides** lists every town, grouped by region, with its neighborhood guides under it. Untick one and save:
- its page returns "not found" to visitors (you can still preview it while logged in) and it leaves the sitemap and llms.txt;
- it drops out of the guide index and region counts, the home page cards, the market table, "nearby" links in other guides, the city's neighborhood list and the footer's town links.

Nothing is deleted, and re-running the importer never changes these switches. Hiding a city hides its neighborhood guides too. The community list also shows an "On site" column.

## Our listings
**Listings > Add listing** in the admin. The title is the address line, the main editor holds the property story, "Main photo" in the sidebar is the lead image, and a **Listing details** box holds:
- status (coming soon, for sale, under agreement, sold), price, street, unit, ZIP
- the town or neighborhood guide it belongs to
- home type, style, beds, baths, square feet, lot, year built
- open house, MLS number, listing brokerage (defaults to REWAP Brokerage LLC), listing agent
- "Feature on the home page", and more photos

Each listing gets its own page at `/listings/<address>/` with the photo gallery, facts, the story, a details table, a map link, a link to its town guide and a "Request a showing" form. Listings also appear:
- on the home page in **Featured homes** (featured ones first)
- on the Buy page in a new **Our listings** section
- in the matching town guide (a neighborhood's listings also show on its city guide)

Sold listings keep their page but leave the grids. Listings work whether or not the IDX switch is on; sections with no listings stay off the page.
