Living in Stoneham, MA

A shoe town's square, a zoo by the pond,and a third of the map left to the Fells.

Stoneham is a Middlesex County town of 23,244 people (2020 Census), less than 10 miles northwest of Boston. About a third of its land lies in the Middlesex Fells Reservation. Slightly more than half its homes are single-family. I-93 runs through town, and Boston-bound rail stations sit in Melrose, Winchester and Malden next door.

About Stoneham

Early explorers called this land Charlestown End, and Governor Winthrop's party is said to have lunched by Spot Pond in 1632. Farmers settled slowly, and the town broke from Charlestown on December 17, 1725. A stagecoach line on the Medford to Andover Turnpike in 1833 brought house lots, and shoemaking grew from home workshops into more than forty manufacturers. That era left Central Square, with its Second Empire commercial blocks, the 1904 library, and churches lining the north side at Common Street.

A few blocks west of the square, Nobility Hill keeps Italianate, Stick and Queen Anne houses from about 1860 to 1920. The Middlesex Fells covers roughly a third of the town, with Spot Pond, Bear Hill and the Stone Zoo, founded in 1905. Stoneham's housing plan counts slightly more than half its homes as single-family, with much of the rest in larger buildings. The Tri-Community Greenway runs from Gould Street toward Woburn and Winchester.

Homes in Stoneham

Stoneham's Housing Production Plan reports that 57.1% of housing units are single-family homes, and nearly half of the remaining multifamily units are in buildings of 20 or more units. About 65% of homes are owner-occupied. The plan points to the town center around Main Street as the area best suited for new housing.

Farm Hill

Getting around

Commuting from Stoneham

Stoneham has no rail station of its own; commuter rail stations in Melrose, Winchester, Wakefield and Woburn serve Boston's North Station, and Oak Grove on the Orange Line is about 3.8 miles from Stoneham Center. I-93 runs through town, I-95/Route 128 passes just to the north, and MBTA bus 132 runs between Redstone Shopping Center and Malden Center Station.

  • Haverhill Line (in neighboring Melrose): Melrose Highlands
  • Lowell Line (in neighboring Winchester): Wedgemere
  • Orange Line (subway, in Malden): Oak Grove

Check current schedules with the MBTA; compare the trip from each address before you buy.

What is the median home price in Stoneham, MA?

According to The Warren Group's MA December 2025 Sales, Prices by Town table, the median single-family sale price in Stoneham for calendar year 2025 was $805,000, across 130 sales. In 2024 it was $784,000, across 140 sales.

Does Stoneham have commuter rail?

No station is located in Stoneham itself. Commuter rail stations in neighboring Melrose, Winchester, Wakefield and Woburn serve Boston's North Station, and Oak Grove on the Orange Line is in Malden.

Which school district serves Stoneham?

Stoneham Public Schools. Review its profile with DESE.
