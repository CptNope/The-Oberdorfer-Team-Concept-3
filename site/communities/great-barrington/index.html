Living in Great Barrington, MA

A Main Street on the Housatonic,and Monument Mountain up Route 7.

Great Barrington is a Berkshire County town of 7,172 people (2020 Census) in the Housatonic River valley. US 7 runs through its center as Main Street, and the town also takes in the villages of Housatonic and Van Deusenville. There is no passenger rail station in town; BRTA buses run to Lee and to Pittsfield, where Amtrak stops. The Warren Group counted 77 single-family sales in Great Barrington in 2025.

About Great Barrington

The Mahican name for this stretch of the river was Mahaiwe, "the place downstream." Colonists settled here in 1726, the area served as the north parish of Sheffield from 1742, and it was incorporated as Great Barrington in 1761. In 1774 local colonists forced the Berkshire County court to close in protest of British policy, and in March 1886 William Stanley's alternating current system lit 23 businesses along Main Street. That Main Street still carries US 7 through the central village, where the Mahaiwe Theater opened in 1905 and reopened in 2005 as a performing arts center.

North of the center, the village of Housatonic grew in the early 1800s around textile and paper mills on the river; Monument Mills, which had 500 employees at its peak, closed in 1956, and the mill buildings are listed on the National Register. Monument Mountain rises along Route 7, with 503 acres and a 1,642-foot summit kept by The Trustees. To the east, East Mountain holds Ski Butternut and a state forest crossed by the Appalachian Trail. The Housatonic River Walk follows the river through downtown, and the W. E. B. Du Bois Boyhood Homesite, a National Historic Landmark, lies on the road to South Egremont.

Homes in Great Barrington

Seasonal use shapes Great Barrington's housing. In December 2025 the Commonwealth identified the town as eligible for its Seasonal Communities designation, whose criteria include the share of housing units used for seasonal, recreational or occasional use. Annual Town Meeting voters accepted the designation on May 2, 2026, and the Selectboard and Planning Board scheduled a joint meeting on related zoning changes for June 1, 2026.

Where you mightlive in Great Barrington.

Housatonic

A village on the town's northern edge, bordering Stockbridge and West Stockbridge, with the river at its center and Route 183 crossing it; it grew around textile and paper mills.

Van Deusenville

One of the town's three named villages, with the central village and Housatonic.

Getting around

Commuting from Great Barrington

Great Barrington has no passenger rail station. The Berkshire Regional Transit Authority runs Route 21 between Great Barrington and Lee and the Route 921 express between Great Barrington and Pittsfield, where Amtrak's Lake Shore Limited stops and the seasonal Berkshire Flyer runs to New York. US 7 is Main Street through the center, Route 23 crosses town west to east, and Route 183 serves the east side and Housatonic. The nearest interstate is the Massachusetts Turnpike (I-90).

Things to doGreat Barrington

Places to knowin Great Barrington.

Monument Mountain

A 503-acre Trustees reservation on Route 7 with three trails to the 1,642-foot Peeskawso Peak, where Hawthorne and Melville once spent an afternoon.

Mahaiwe Performing Arts Center

The theater at 14 Castle Street opened on September 26, 1905, and reopened after restoration on May 29, 2005.

W. E. B. Du Bois Boyhood Homesite

A 5-acre National Historic Landmark (1976) on Routes 23 and 41, stewarded by UMass Amherst, with a path to a kiosk, a memorial boulder and the old house's foundation.

Housatonic River Walk

A national recreation trail along the river in downtown, a project of the Great Barrington Land Conservancy, open free in daylight from spring through fall.

Ski Butternut

A ski area on East Mountain with 22 trails and about 1,000 feet of vertical drop.

What is the median home price in Great Barrington, MA?

According to The Warren Group's MA December 2025 Sales, Prices by Town table, the median single-family sale price in Great Barrington for calendar year 2025 was $575,000, across 77 sales. In 2024 it was $550,000, across 69 sales.

Does Great Barrington have train service?

No passenger rail station is located in Great Barrington. BRTA's Route 921 express bus connects the town with Pittsfield, where Amtrak's Lake Shore Limited stops and the seasonal Berkshire Flyer runs to New York.

Which school district serves Great Barrington?

Berkshire Hills Regional School District, the regional K-12 district for Great Barrington, Stockbridge and West Stockbridge. Review its profile with DESE.
