Find your rhythm

Lakeside moments

Lake Quannapowitt is one of Wakefield's signature gathering places. Its shoreline offers a setting for walking, running, and community events.

A town center

The Common, Main Street, and Albion Street help anchor downtown life. Walk the area, browse the local businesses, and see how the streets fit into your day.

Room to explore

Wakefield and Greenwood offer commuter rail access. Compare the trip from each home, then explore nearby Reading, Stoneham, and Melrose.
