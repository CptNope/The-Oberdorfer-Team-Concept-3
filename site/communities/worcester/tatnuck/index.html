Living in Tatnuck, Worcester, MA

A square where Pleasant and Chandler meet,at the end of the old streetcar line.

Tatnuck is a neighborhood on Worcester's West Side centered on Tatnuck Square, where Pleasant and Chandler Streets meet. It was a small cluster of houses by about 1830 and grew after the streetcar reached it along Pleasant Street in 1899. WRTA Route 2 still ends at Tatnuck Square, which has a city fire station, a branch library and a church, and Liberty Farm, a National Historic Landmark, stands on Mower Street.

About Tatnuck

Where it is

Tatnuck is on Worcester's West Side around Tatnuck Square, the intersection of Pleasant and Chandler Streets. West Tatnuck, which Wikipedia lists as a separate West Side neighborhood, is where the Tatnuck streetcar line was carried in 1913 and where WRTA Route 6 runs via Chandler Street.

Tatnuck's name is older than the city. A Nipmuc place-name list gives 'at the great hill' as the meaning of Tatnit, listing Tatnuck as a variant spelling, and the Massachusetts Historical Commission's survey of Worcester records settlement along Tatnuck Brook in the west and suggests that a village at Tatnuck served as a gathering place before English settlement. By about 1830 the survey counts Tatnuck, at the Pleasant and Chandler Street intersection, among the small residential clusters on the edge of the town, and between 1830 and 1870 it lists Tatnuck among the places where textile mills were built on Worcester's streams.

The streetcar turned the crossroads into a square. The Worcester Consolidated Street Railway opened an extension of its Pleasant Street line to Tatnuck on May 30, 1899, pushed it 691 feet farther west in 1904, and carried it another 4,668 feet to West Tatnuck in August 1913. The route has survived as a bus: WRTA Route 2 runs to Tatnuck Square via Pleasant Street, and Route 6 runs to West Tatnuck via Chandler Street. Around the square on Pleasant Street stand the city's Tatnuck Square fire station at 1067, the First Congregational Church at 1070, where a monthly Tatnuck Square neighborhood meeting has been held, and the Worcester Public Library's Tatnuck Magnet Branch at 1083.

The neighborhood's best-known house predates the streetcar by almost a century. Liberty Farm, at 116 Mower Street, is a Federal-style farmhouse of about 1810 where the abolitionists and suffrage campaigners Abby Kelley Foster and Stephen Symonds Foster lived for most of their married life; they sheltered people escaping slavery and refused to pay property taxes because Abby could not vote. It was designated a National Historic Landmark in 1974 and remains a private residence.

Getting around

Commuting from Tatnuck

Tatnuck has no rail station; the nearest is Worcester station at Union Station, 2 Washington Square, downtown, the western end of the MBTA Framingham/Worcester Line. WRTA Route 2 runs between downtown and Tatnuck Square via Pleasant Street, and Route 6 runs to West Tatnuck via Chandler Street.

The Worcester city guide

What is the median home price in Tatnuck, Worcester?

The Warren Group does not report Tatnuck separately. Citywide, the median single-family sale price in Worcester for calendar year 2025 was $430,000 across 884 sales, up from $420,000 across 904 sales in 2024, according to its MA December 2025 Sales, Prices by Town report.

How do you get from Tatnuck to downtown Worcester without a car?

WRTA Route 2 runs between downtown and Tatnuck Square via Pleasant Street, following the streetcar line that reached Tatnuck in 1899, and Route 6 serves West Tatnuck via Chandler Street. Commuter rail leaves from Worcester station at Union Station, downtown, on the MBTA Framingham/Worcester Line.

Which school district serves Tatnuck?

Worcester Public Schools. School assignment is by address and is set by the district; review the district's profile with DESE.

Where is Tatnuck Square?

Tatnuck Square is the intersection of Pleasant and Chandler Streets on Worcester's West Side, according to the Massachusetts Historical Commission's survey of the city. The Tatnuck Square fire station is at 1067 Pleasant Street.

Can you visit Liberty Farm in Tatnuck?

No. Liberty Farm, the c. 1810 home of Abby Kelley Foster and Stephen Symonds Foster at 116 Mower Street, is a National Historic Landmark but a private residence that is not open to the public.
