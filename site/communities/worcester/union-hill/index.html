Living in Union Hill, Worcester, MA

A school campus, a synagogue and three-deckerson Providence Street's hill.

Union Hill is an East Side neighborhood in southeastern Worcester, on the hill once called Sagatabscot, one of the city's seven hills. Providence Street holds the Worcester Academy campus, on the hill since 1869, and the 1906 Shaarai Torah Synagogue, and the city's 1977-78 historic survey names Union Hill among the places built up with three-deckers. WRTA Route 1 runs via Providence Street, and Union Station is downtown.

About Union Hill

Where it is

Union Hill has no official city boundary. Wikipedia lists it as an East Side neighborhood on the hill anchored by Grafton Street and Providence Street in southeastern Worcester, and names Massasoit Road as spanning Union Hill, Grafton Hill and Broadmeadow Brook; its documented landmarks stand on Providence Street (numbers 32 and 81) and Chapin Street.

Union Hill is one of the hills Worcester grew up onto in the 19th century. Wikipedia counts it, under its old name Sagatabscot, among the seven hills the city government lists, and notes that it is also called Grafton Hill. The Massachusetts Historical Commission's survey of Worcester records that between 1830 and 1870 the town spread east up Chandler, Oak and Union Hills, and that Worcester Academy operated in town until its relocation to Union Hill. That move came in 1869, when Isaac Davis, the academy's first president, bought the abandoned Dale General Hospital on Providence Street, a building designed by Elbridge Boyden in 1852 for a medical school and used as a Civil War military hospital, and gave it to the school as Davis Hall.

The campus filled in over the next two decades. The academy's National Register nomination calls Walker Hall (1889, Fuller & Delano) the first substantial building designed for the school on its Union Hill campus; Adams and Dexter Halls followed in 1892 and Kingsley Laboratories (Barker & Nourse) in 1897, and the campus was listed on the National Register as the Worcester Academy Historic District in 1980. Davis Hall lost a tower in the 1938 hurricane and was demolished in 1965. Down Providence Street, at number 32, the Shaarai Torah congregation opened a Classical Revival synagogue designed by Edwin T. Chapin on September 14, 1906. The survey behind Worcester's 1980 Multiple Resource Area nomination names Union Hill among the places where whole new neighborhoods of three-deckers were built in the period it dates 1890 to 1950.

In August 2015 the city announced the Union Hill Neighborhood Revitalization Initiative, committing $4 million in federal Community Development Block Grant funds to housing rehabilitation and code enforcement, new sidewalks, road resurfacing and school health partnerships, with Oak Hill Community Development Corporation, Habitat for Humanity, Worcester Academy and HUD as partners. The city outlined the program at a press conference on August 17, 2015, at Worcester Academy's Alumni House, 51 Providence Street, and in November 2015 it received the National Community Development Association's Community Development Achievement Award. The academy's Walker Hall hosted a Union Hill neighborhood meeting on the city's calendar in June 2025.

Homes in Union Hill

The survey for Worcester's 1977-78 Multiple Resource Area National Register nomination names Union Hill among the places where whole new neighborhoods of three-deckers were built, in the period it dates 1890 to 1950; it did not nominate individual three-deckers because of their number. Housing and code enforcement was one of the three focus areas of the city's 2015 Union Hill initiative, whose projects included housing rehabs. No published count of housing types or ownership for Union Hill alone, with a dated source and a stated boundary, was found.

Getting around

Commuting from Union Hill

Union Hill has no rail station of its own; the nearest is Worcester station at Union Station, 2 Washington Square, downtown, on the MBTA Framingham/Worcester Line. WRTA Route 1 (Walmart 146 via Providence and Granite Streets) runs via Providence Street, and Routes 5 and 12 run on Grafton Street.

  • Framingham/Worcester Line: Worcester (Union Station, 2 Washington Square)

Check current schedules with the MBTA and the WRTA; compare the trip from each address before you buy.

Schools

Public schools

Union Hill is served by Worcester Public Schools. School assignment depends on the address and the district's own rules; confirm it with the district, and review its profile with the Massachusetts Department of Elementary and Secondary Education.

Places to knowin Union Hill.

Worcester Academy Historic District

The campus of Worcester Academy, founded in 1834, at 81 Providence Street; the academy bought its Union Hill site in 1869 and the campus was listed on the National Register on March 5, 1980.

Walker Hall

Built in 1889 to a design by Fuller & Delano, the first substantial building on the Union Hill campus designed for Worcester Academy, according to its National Register nomination; it hosted a city-listed Union Hill neighborhood meeting in June 2025.

Shaarai Torah Synagogue

A Classical Revival building at 32 Providence Street by Edwin T. Chapin, opened on September 14, 1906 and listed on the National Register in 1990; after a 2003 sale it was planned for conversion into 11 apartments known as Red Oak Condominiums.

Site of Dale General Hospital

Elbridge Boyden's 1852 building on Providence Street served as a Civil War military hospital in 1864 and 1865, became Worcester Academy's Davis Hall in 1869 and was demolished in 1965; the Davol Hall dormitory was later built on roughly the same site.

Union Hill School

A Worcester Public Schools building at 1 Chapin Street, where student volunteers with Working for Worcester and staff from its sponsors, including Consigli Construction, built a new playground.

The Worcester city guide

What is the median home price in Union Hill, Worcester?

The Warren Group does not report Union Hill separately. Citywide, the median single-family sale price in Worcester for calendar year 2025 was $430,000 across 884 sales, up from $420,000 across 904 sales in 2024, according to its MA December 2025 Sales, Prices by Town report.

Is there commuter rail near Union Hill?

Union Hill has no station of its own. The nearest is Worcester station at Union Station, 2 Washington Square, downtown, on the MBTA Framingham/Worcester Line. WRTA Route 1 runs via Providence Street.

Which school district serves Union Hill?

Worcester Public Schools, which runs the Union Hill School at 1 Chapin Street. School assignment is by address and is set by the district; review the district's profile with DESE.

Is Union Hill the same as Grafton Hill?

The names overlap. Wikipedia's Grafton Hill article says Union Hill is also called Grafton Hill and was once Sagatabscot Hill, while its list of Worcester neighborhoods names Union Hill, Grafton Hill and Vernon Hill as separate East Side neighborhoods.

What was the Union Hill revitalization initiative?

A city program announced in August 2015 that committed $4 million in federal Community Development Block Grant funds to housing rehabilitation and code enforcement, new sidewalks, road resurfacing and school health partnerships, with Oak Hill CDC, Habitat for Humanity, Worcester Academy and HUD as partners. It received a national Community Development Achievement Award in November 2015.
