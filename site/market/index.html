What these numbers can tell you

The townwide median is a starting point. Your best comparisons are recent closed sales that resemble your property in location, size, condition, and style. A condo, an older single-family home, and a newly built residence can tell very different stories.

Market reports for other Massachusetts communities will be added as the team publishes them, each with its own source and date.

Sold vs. asking

A listing price is a seller's starting point. A closed sale shows what a buyer actually paid.

Beyond square feet

Lot size, renovation quality, layout, parking, and condominium fees all belong in the comparison.
