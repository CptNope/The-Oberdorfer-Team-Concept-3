Information on this site

The site is here to help you learn about buying and selling in Massachusetts and to reach Brandon and Kaitlyn Oberdorfer. It is general information, not legal, tax, financial, lending, inspection or appraisal advice. For those, please talk to a qualified professional.

  • Market figures show their source, period and place. They describe the past and may be revised by their source; they do not predict what a home will sell for.

  • Town and neighborhood guides describe places, housing, transit, history and amenities, with a source for each fact. Towns, school districts and transit agencies change their plans, so check with them for the latest.
  • Testimonials are a client's own words, quoted with their permission and linked to where they first wrote them. Every sale is different, and past results do not promise future ones.

We work to keep the site accurate and up to date, but we cannot promise that every page is complete or current at every moment.

Browsing the site, sending a form or booking a call does not by itself create an agency or client relationship with The Oberdorfer Team or REWAP Brokerage LLC. If we work together, the relationship is agreed directly with you, with the disclosures and agreements Massachusetts law requires. Until then, please do not send us anything you would want kept confidential in a negotiation.

Contacting us and booking a call

  • Please give accurate contact details, and only your own, or someone else's with their permission.
  • Calls booked online are with the agent you chose, at the time shown in your confirmation. An agent may occasionally need to move or cancel a call; if so, they will tell you. Your confirmation email has a private link to move or cancel it yourself.
  • Sending a form or booking a call does not sign you up for marketing email. We send market updates or newsletters only if you tick the box to receive them.
  • Please do not send Social Security numbers, bank or card details through the site.

How we handle what you send us is explained in our privacy policy.
