How do I contact Kaitlyn Oberdorfer?

Call or text The Oberdorfer Team at 406-250-7620, or send a message from this page. Messages sent here go to Kaitlyn and are attributed to Kaitlyn in the team's lead routing.

Do Kaitlyn and Brandon work together?

Yes. Kaitlyn leads The Oberdorfer Team with her husband, Brandon Oberdorfer. The team describes itself as “Husband. Wife. Your Team.”: two partners invested in your goals.

Which areas does Kaitlyn Oberdorfer serve?

Kaitlyn helps buyers and sellers across Massachusetts. The team works from Worcester, and its community guides run from Central Massachusetts and MetroWest to Boston, the South Shore, Cape Cod, the South Coast and the Berkshires, including Wakefield, where the team publishes Wakefield at Home.

Which brokerage is Kaitlyn Oberdorfer with?

Kaitlyn is part of The Oberdorfer Team, a real estate team operating under REWAP Brokerage LLC in Massachusetts.
