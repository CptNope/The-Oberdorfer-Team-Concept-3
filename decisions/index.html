AI search crawlers

Whether AI tools such as ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity and Gemini may read the site. The site's robots.txt states the choice at launch. Google's AI Overviews use the ordinary Google crawler, so they are not affected.

Decides
Brandon and Kaitlyn
Holds up
robots.txt at launch
Recommended

Allow them

  • The town guides are written to be quoted, with sources on every fact
  • Answers that cite the team link back to the site

Block AI training, allow AI search

  • Block training crawlers such as GPTBot and Google-Extended; allow search crawlers such as OAI-SearchBot and PerplexityBot
  • Keeps citations, limits reuse of the writing

Block them all

  • The content stays out of those tools' answers
  • Ordinary Google search is not affected
