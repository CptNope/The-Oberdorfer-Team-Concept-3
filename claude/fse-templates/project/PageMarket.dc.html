Massachusetts housing market insights

Know the market.Love the home.

The statewide median single-family sale price was $638,000 in 2025, up 3.7% from 2024, and the condominium median was $537,000, according to The Warren Group. Town by town, the picture varies widely.

What these numbers can tell you

A statewide or townwide median is a starting point. Your best comparisons are recent closed sales that resemble your property in location, size, condition, and style. A condo, an older single-family home, and a newly built residence can tell very different stories.
