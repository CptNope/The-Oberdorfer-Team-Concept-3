The best plansare close to home.

Six ways to enjoy Wakefield and the surrounding area this season.

pattern post-content · core/columns (article + aside) › core/post-content (caption, lede, ot/guide-list, sources) · aside: publication meta, related links, PDF download

From Wakefield at Home, Fall 2026 · Seasonal features · Photo: Joseph Reagle / Wikimedia Commons, CC BY-SA 4.0, cropped.

A little fresh air. A few good memories. The best autumn plans rarely need a long drive.

  1. Walk the lake

    Choose a stretch of the Lake Quannapowitt shoreline, bring a warm drink, and leave time to pause at the Common.

    wakefieldma.gov

  2. Shop the harvest

    Wakefield Farmers Market: Saturdays, 9 AM–1 PM, through October 31 at Hall Park, 468 North Avenue.

    wakefieldfarmersmarket.org

  3. Follow the foliage

    Breakheart Reservation offers forest trails and lakes. The Wakefield entrance is at 100 Hemlock Road; choose a route that suits your group.

    mass.gov / Breakheart Reservation

  4. Make a library day

    Browse Beebe Library's programs or reserve an eligible museum pass. A rainy day can become a very good plan.

    wakefieldlibrary.org

  5. Pick an autumn favorite

    Nearby Brooksby Farm in Peabody offers seasonal apple picking, cider donuts, and a farm store. Check crop availability before heading over.

    brooksbyfarm.org

  6. Get lost in the fun

    Connors Farm in Danvers offers a fall corn maze and daytime farm activities. Check admission, hours, and weather updates before your visit.

    connorsfarm.com

Seasonal schedules, crops, and access can change. Check each destination before you go. Listed details as published in October 2026.
