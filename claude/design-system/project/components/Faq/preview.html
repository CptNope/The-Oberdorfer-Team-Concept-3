How do I start the buying process?

Begin with a lender conversation about preapproval and a comfortable monthly budget. The Consumer Financial Protection Bureau's guide to preapproval letters is a useful first read.

What is a property-specific market review?

A comparison of recent closed sales that resemble your home in location, size, condition and style. It is more useful than a townwide median.
