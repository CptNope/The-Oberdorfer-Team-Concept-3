If you are selling

  1. Start with the right comparisons.

    Review recent closed sales with similar location, style, condition, and living area.

  2. Create a strong first impression.

    Clear the entry, simplify the rooms, and show the home in good light.

  3. Build a plan around your life.

    Discuss your timing, showing preferences, and next move.

If you are buying

  1. Get ready before you find the one.

    Connect with a lender for preapproval and review a comfortable monthly budget.

  2. Know your everyday priorities.

    Test the routine as well as the floor plan.

  3. Look beyond the listing price.

    Consider taxes, insurance, condominium fees, and maintenance.
