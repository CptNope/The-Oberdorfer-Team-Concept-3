Rules

Two lines that answer

Headlines are often two short sentences, the second answering the first. Break the line where the thought turns, using .answer.

Light is reserved

Use weight 300 only from 30px up. Smaller serif text uses 400–500, and body text never goes below Manrope 400.

Capitals are small

Uppercase is limited to sans labels and buttons, tracked 0.14–0.18em. Never set a serif headline in all caps, except the stacked logo.

The magazine's whitespace, on Concept B's architectural twelve-column grid.

Grid

12 columns, gutter 16 → 32px, page margin 16 → 80px, content max 1312px. Editorial layouts favour 6 + 5 offsets (image left, text from column 8).

Lines

Square corners everywhere. Depth comes from rules and grounds, almost never shadows.

.rule: 1px antique gold. Section and column dividers, as in the magazine.

.rule--hair: softened gold for long dividers on ivory.

.dim-rule: dimension line with end ticks, taken from the house drawing on page 7. Use it for measurements, ranges and architectural sections.

.folio-frame: the cover's inset double frame. Use one per view, on covers, heroes and the closing panel.

BehaviourDesign System

18

One gesture:the line draws.

The brand moves the way a pen rules a page. Gold lines draw from left to right and content settles beneath them. There is no parallax, bounce or looping animation.

Motion tokens

Rule draw · --dur-reveal 1100ms · --ease-out cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)

--dur-fast160msColour, border
--dur-base280msButton rule, nav underline
--dur-slow720msSection settle, menu wipe
--dur-reveal1100msGold rule draw, image ease-in on hover

Accessibility floor

Target: WCAG 2.2 AA.

Perceivable

Contrast is checked for every pairing above. Photos carry alt text, and decorative linework is aria-hidden. Content is visible by default if JavaScript fails.

Operable

2px focus rings with a 3px offset in navy, or pale gold on dark grounds. Targets are at least 44px. A skip link, Escape to close panels, and no keyboard traps. prefers-reduced-motion removes all movement.

Understandable

Labels sit above fields. Errors name the problem and the fix, and statuses are announced. Buttons say what they do, and forms say what happens next.
